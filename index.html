Dominik Bittner

PhD Researcher in Environmental Sustainability, University of Aberdeen · Aberdeen, UK

My bond with nature began at an early age, growing up on a farm with its woodlands. I have witnessed firsthand the challenges faced by farmers due to deteriorating soil health, leading to a strong motivation to advance sustainable agricultural practices. Fully in line with UN Sustainable Development Goals, I am particularly invested in helping regions where support is urgently needed, such as in sub-Saharan Africa, where farmers heavily depend on the health of their soils.

My PhD combines biophysical process models, machine learning and expert knowledge into a hybrid soil engine, used to assess how nature-based solutions can restore degraded soils and support smallholder farmers in Ethiopia. Earlier work looked at eye tracking and deep learning for adaptive learning systems, and computer vision for forestry and wildfire prevention.

Research interests: hybrid AI, soil health, sustainable agriculture, environmental modelling, machine learning, computer vision.
